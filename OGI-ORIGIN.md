# OGI: Off Grid Intelligence

## Authorship and origin record

**Creator and product originator: Chirag Mirani.** I conceived this project to bring offline artificial intelligence, survival information and practical field tools together, with the purpose of helping save lives. I see enormous potential for defense and emergency response.
